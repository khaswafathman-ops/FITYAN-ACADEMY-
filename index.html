About FITYAN Academy

Learning, Character & Purpose

FITYAN is a youth-focused educational initiative dedicated to nurturing confident, knowledgeable and values-driven young people.

Who We Are

FITYAN provides engaging, interactive and practical learning experiences that combine authentic Islamic teachings with skills young people can apply in their everyday lives.

The Academy focuses on developing young people intellectually, socially, spiritually and practically through workshops, programs, discussions, activities and other learning experiences.

Our Approach

We believe young people learn best when they participate, ask questions, discuss real situations, reflect on their choices and practise what they learn.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the programs only the ones listed here?

No. The listed programs represent FITYAN Academy's current learning areas. We also conduct other programs related to life skills and youth development and can consider requests for additional topics.

How long does a program take?

Program duration is arranged according to the needs of the participants and the requirements of the program.

Can a school or organization request a specific program?

Yes. Schools, organizations, youth groups and other participants can use the registration form to communicate their needs or request a specific topic.

How do I register?

Click any Register button on this website. It will open the FITYAN Academy Google Form.
